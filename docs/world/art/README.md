## The short version

- **Top recommendation: a hybrid.** Painted portraits, painted halls and painted backdrops where you stop and look. 3D and code under your feet.
- **The trade you cannot avoid:** art very close to your photos, or a camera free to go anywhere. Free tools on a phone cannot give both in the same place.
- **Free picture tools are strong. Free picture-to-3D tools are much weaker than they look,** and there is no confirmed free rigging route from an iPhone.
- **Test first.** The phone test in file 7 costs an hour and no money, and it tells you whether any of this reaches your bar.

Keep everything else about the app exactly the same.
