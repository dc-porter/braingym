# 1. Art style guide for Aurelune

Nov 16, 2026 · for Dee · Phase 3, Task A

This is your art brief, expanded into rules an artist, an art tool or a later model can follow. It is written from your 12 reference photos. The photos are mood guides only. Nothing here copies or traces any of them, and none of them goes into the project.

It replaces the character and building parts of `docs/world/03-art-direction.md` and `04-characters.md`. The sky, water, light and motion rules there still stand.

**Two things I noticed that you should know:**

1. **Your people references are in three different rendering styles:** photo-real, soft illustrated and comic-book line art. A world looks designed when every character is painted the same way. You need to pick one. My recommendation is below and in file 9.
2. **Several references carry creator marks,** and most are labeled AI-made. That is fine for mood. It is one more reason never to feed them into an art tool as a starting image. Describe in words, always.

## The look in one line

**A heavenly city of crystal and pearl, floating above a sea of cloud, lit from within, where every person looks painted by the same master.**

## The world

**Light.** Soft and bright. Light seems to come from everywhere: from inside the crystal, off the water, through the haze. Long visible sun rays. Shadows are pale lilac, never black.

**Color.** Starting values, to tune on screen.

| Name | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Pearl white | #F6F2FA | Marble, terraces, the tiered city |
| Opal pink | #F3C6DC | Cloud sea, crystal glints, dawn |
| Blossom | #E79CC4 | Trees, flowers, fabric accents |
| Lilac | #B9A6E8 | Shadows, far islands, glass |
| Crystal teal | #7FD9D6 | Water, crystal cores, glass edges |
| Periwinkle sky | #8FA3E0 | Daytime sky |
| Coral cloud | #F2A58E | Sunset cloud edges |
| Deep twilight | #2B2466 | Night sky, the calm task card |
| Sun gold | #FFD98A | Warm inner light, lit windows, coins |

**Four moods, by your phone's clock.** Dawn is pink and gold. Day is pearl white and periwinkle, the default. Dusk is coral and violet. Night is deep twilight with the great planet and glowing crystal.

**Sky.** A large moon or planet always in view, with a thin crescent near it. A sea of soft cloud below the islands in place of a flat ocean. Floating rock islands in the far distance, each with trees on top and a thin waterfall falling into the cloud.

**Land.** The islands you walk on are pearl terraces with pools, low steps and gardens. Edges are clean stone with a gold line.

**Water.** Still and mirror-like in pools. Long, thin and misty in waterfalls. It reflects the sky and the towers.

**Plants.** Blossom trees in pink and white. Low flower beds in pink, violet and white. Clusters of small glowing crystals growing among the flowers. A few tall palms in the tiered city.

**How the world grows.** It starts as one small crystal court. With each place you light it gains a terrace, a pool or a tower. Fully grown, it reads as the white tiered city: wide, layered, with small floating monuments in the distance.

**Motion.** Slow. Drifting cloud, falling water, shimmering crystal, floating petals and light motes.

## Buildings

- **Shape:** tall and slender. Clusters of spires of different heights, like organ pipes or crystal growth. Pointed arches. Fluted columns.
- **Material:** clear or milky crystal and glass with rainbow glints at the edges (iridescent). Pearl marble for floors, steps and bases. Thin gold for frames, capitals and tracery.
- **Light:** every building glows softly from inside. Doorways are the brightest point.
- **Detail:** facets, ribs, tracery and tiers. No large blank surfaces. This is the difference between "designed" and "blocks".
- **Scale:** small human, enormous building.
- **Not this:** flat-colored cylinders and cones, thick chunky walls, cartoon outlines.

## Interiors

Each place you enter is a grand hall seen from the doorway.

- A long view to a bright focal point: a rose window, a throne of light, a glowing arch.
- Two rows of fluted white columns with gold capitals.
- A ceiling that shows the sky or a galaxy.
- Living things indoors: blossom trees, flower urns, a channel of water.
- A polished floor that mirrors everything.
- Each host's hall gets its own accent color and one signature object, so you know where you are at a glance.

## Characters

### One rendering style for everyone

**Recommended: painterly realism.** Real proportions and real faces, painted with soft brushwork, like a high-end fantasy game portrait. It is the style of your white-gown, white-afro reference.

Why this and not photo-real: photo-real faces drift toward looking like real people, which your rules forbid. They also sit oddly inside a glowing fantasy world, and small flaws in them look disturbing. Painterly realism forgives more, matches the world and is the style free art tools do most reliably.

### Rules for every character

- **Adults,** elegant and composed. Calm, confident expressions.
- **Faces:** open, expressive eyes. Real features. No two characters share a face.
- **Skin for the Lumen folk:** warm brown tones with golden undertones. Your references run deeper than the three color values recorded in the first style guide. See decision 5 in file 9.
- **Hair:** long platinum, white or pale gold, in many textures: straight with braids, box braids, locs, loose waves, a full afro. Hair is detailed, with visible strands and ornaments.
- **Eyes:** natural browns and ambers for most. Violet or pale blue with a faint glow for a few, as a mark of a gift.
- **Ears:** softly pointed, small. One shared trait that says "of Aurelune".
- **Pose:** standing tall, facing the viewer or three-quarters. Hands visible and relaxed.
- **Dress code:** regal and covered. Floor-length gowns, robes, wraps and cloaks. Your earlier design already called for floor-length robes, and Bloom welcomes ages 15 and up, so I am leaving out the bare midriffs and high slits that appear in some references.
- **Original only.** No real person, no franchise character, no named artist's style.

One short note on the Star Guardian: I would design her dress from invented Aurelune culture (constellation patterns, star-marks) and not ask an art tool for "tribal". That word makes art tools produce stereotypes.

## Clothing and jewelry

- **Cut:** draped and layered. Greek-inspired wraps and gowns, long open robes, high collars for the court, a cloak or a drape over one shoulder.
- **Cloth:** silk and velvet with a soft sheen. Opal and iridescent cloth for the court. Fine embroidery along edges.
- **Jewelry:** circlets, collars, layered fine chains, cuffs, gems set at the brow and the belt. Gold for the Lumen folk. Silver and pearl for the Veil folk and Tideborn.
- **Not this:** armor plates, helmets, capes with clasps, lightning marks or anything that reads as a costume from a known film or game.

**About "gold means earned".** The first style guide reserved gold for things you have lit. Your references are full of gold jewelry and trim, so that rule needs sharpening: **gold metal is decoration, and warm golden light is "earned".** A place you have restored glows gold from inside. An unlit place has the same gold trim but no inner light.

## The style lock

Art tools drift. To keep everything looking like one world, every request for art starts with the same sentence and ends with the same sentence.

**Open every character request with:**
> Painterly realism, high-end fantasy game character art, soft brushwork, detailed face, hair and fabric, soft luminous light with a cool rim light, elegant and regal.

**Open every place request with:**
> Painterly fantasy concept art of a heavenly crystal city above a sea of clouds, luminous and ethereal, pearl white, opal pink, lilac and teal, light glowing from within the crystal, soft sun rays, highly detailed.

**Close every request with:**
> Original design. No text, no logo, no watermark, no signature.

**Never put in a request:** the name of a real person, a film, a show, a game, a studio or an artist. Not "Targaryen", not "Asgardian", not "in the style of". Describe what you see instead: "long silver-white hair", "draped Greek-inspired gown". Names make tools copy.

**Never upload a reference photo into an art tool.** Words only. Uploading is the tool-assisted version of tracing.
