# 7. A phone test you can run in under an hour

Nov 16, 2026 · for Dee

**Purpose:** find out, with your own eyes and before anything is built, whether free tools can make art that reaches your bar. One character and one place, through the top free route.

**What you need:** your iPhone and a Google account. Nothing to install if you use Safari. Nothing to pay. If any step asks for a card or a paid plan, stop and skip that step.

**Do the steps one at a time.** Each part ends with a short scorecard.

**Two rules while you test**

- **Do not upload any of your reference photos** into the tool. Words only.
- **Do not add names** of shows, films, games, artists or real people to the requests. The requests below already describe what you want.

---

## Part 1: one character (about 25 minutes)

The tool is the **Google Gemini app.** Its free plan includes making and editing pictures (checked Nov 16, 2026). Google does not publish how many a day. If it says you have reached a limit, switch to ChatGPT or Microsoft Copilot and paste the same words. I could not confirm their free limits.

**Step 1.** Open the Gemini app, or go to gemini.google.com in Safari, and sign in.

**Step 2.** Start a new chat. Copy this whole block and send it:

> Create an image. Painterly realism, high-end fantasy game character art, soft brushwork, detailed face, hair and fabric, soft luminous light with a cool rim light, elegant and regal. Full-body painting, head to toe, of an original fantasy queen standing and facing the viewer. Warm brown skin with golden undertones. Very long platinum-white hair past her waist, with fine braids at the temples. Small softly pointed ears. A thin crescent-moon circlet. A layered floor-length gown that fades from twilight purple at the shoulders to rose at the hem, with fine gold trim. Calm, warm expression, eyes open. Hands relaxed at her sides. Plain pale gray background with nothing else in the picture. Tall picture, 9:16. Original design. No text, no logo, no watermark, no signature.

**Step 3.** Look at the result. If something is off, reply in the same chat with one plain fix at a time, for example:

- "Keep everything the same, but make her skin a deeper warm brown."
- "Keep everything the same, but show her whole body including the hem of the gown."
- "Keep everything the same, but fix her hands. Five fingers each, relaxed."

Allow yourself three fixes at most. If it is not good after three, that is a result too.

**Step 4.** Press and hold the best picture and save it to Photos.

**Step 5.** In the same chat, send:

> Using the same woman, the same face, the same hair and the same gown: paint a head-and-shoulders portrait. She is turned slightly to one side and looks at the viewer with a gentle smile. A soft, out-of-focus crystal hall behind her. Square picture. No text, no watermark.

Save it.

**Step 6.** In the same chat, send:

> Using the same woman and the same gown: paint her full body seen from directly behind, head to toe. Plain pale gray background. Tall picture, 9:16. No text, no watermark.

Save it.

**Step 7, optional: try a cutout.** Open the full-body picture in Photos. Press and hold on the queen until a glowing outline runs around her, then let go. Tap **Share**. If you see **Save Image**, tap it. (Apple's page confirms the press-and-hold and the Share option on iOS 16 and later. The Save Image step is from other guides.) If it does not work, skip it. A cloud run can remove a plain background.

### Scorecard for the character

Hold the phone at a normal distance. Answer yes or no.

| # | Question |
|---|---|
| 1 | Does the full-body picture look like a game artist designed it? |
| 2 | Is the face lifelike and pleasant, with nothing odd about the eyes? |
| 3 | Is the skin tone the one you asked for, and the same in all three pictures? |
| 4 | Do the hair and the gown have the detail you want? |
| 5 | Is it clearly the same woman in the portrait and from behind? |
| 6 | Are the hands acceptable, or easily hidden? |
| 7 | Now pinch the full-body picture down until she is about as tall as your thumbnail. Does she still read as elegant and detailed? |

**Pass:** yes on 1, 2 and 3, and on at least two of the rest. Question 7 matters because that is her real size when she stands in the world.

## Part 2: one place (about 15 minutes)

**Step 8.** Start a **new** chat. Send:

> Create an image. Painterly fantasy concept art of a heavenly crystal city above a sea of clouds, luminous and ethereal, pearl white, opal pink, lilac and teal, light glowing from within the crystal, soft sun rays, highly detailed. The inside of a vast luminous hall seen from its doorway: two rows of tall white fluted columns with gold capitals, a glowing rose window of pale glass at the far end, a ceiling like a teal and violet galaxy, pink blossom trees growing indoors along both sides, and a polished floor that mirrors everything. No people. Tall picture, 9:16. Original design. No text, no logo, no watermark, no signature.

Save the best one. Up to three fixes, as before.

**Step 9.** In the same chat, send:

> Create an image in the same style. A sea of soft pink and lilac cloud at dusk. Floating rock islands with trees on top and long thin waterfalls falling into the cloud. A huge pale moon low in the sky with a thin crescent beside it. Far away, a cluster of slender glowing crystal spires. No people. Tall picture, 9:16. Original design. No text, no logo, no watermark, no signature.

Save it.

### Scorecard for the place

| # | Question |
|---|---|
| 1 | Does the hall feel like your interior reference: grand, tall, luminous? |
| 2 | Does the far view feel like your floating-rocks and moon references? |
| 3 | Do the two pictures look like the same world? |
| 4 | Would you be happy to stand in front of either one? |

**Pass:** three of four.

## Part 3, optional: one try at 3D (about 15 minutes)

This part uses tools I could **not** confirm work on a phone. If a page does not load, will not take your picture or asks for money, stop. "It does not work from an iPhone" is a useful answer.

**Step 10.** In Safari, try the first of these that opens:

- **Tencent Hunyuan 3D:** 3d.hunyuanglobal.com. Reported to give about 20 free models a day. Not checked.
- **TRELLIS.2 on Hugging Face:** huggingface.co/spaces/microsoft/TRELLIS.2. A free demo of an open model. It needs a free Hugging Face account and gives a few tries a day.

**Step 11.** Sign in if asked. Upload your full-body picture from Part 1, the one you made, never a reference. Start it and wait. It can take a few minutes.

**Step 12.** When the model appears, drag to turn it. Look at the face, the hair, the hands and the back. Take two screenshots: one from the front, one from the side.

### Scorecard for 3D

| # | Question |
|---|---|
| 1 | From the front, at thumbnail size, does it look like your painting? |
| 2 | Up close, is the face acceptable? |
| 3 | Does the long hair look like hair? |
| 4 | Does the back look right? |

**How to read it:** yes on 1 only means 3D could work for small figures in the world and nothing more. Yes on all four would change my recommendation.

---

## What to do with the results

1. Put the saved pictures and screenshots in one album.
2. Start a session and attach the best four or five: the full body, the portrait, the back view, the hall and the far view.
3. Tell me the scorecard answers and anything that annoyed you.

**The real test comes next.** A picture that looks lovely in Photos can still sit badly in the scene. With your go-ahead, one small run would place these pictures in the prototype, so you can judge Amaris as a painted figure in a painted hall on the preview link. That run is small.

## If the test fails

- **Character fails, place passes:** paint the places, and decide whether to keep simple figures or find one human artist for the cast.
- **Both fail:** free tools do not reach your bar. The honest options are then a small paid tool or a human artist. That breaks your free-only rule, so it would be your call, not mine.
- **Both pass:** the hybrid route is on, and file 9 lists what to decide.
