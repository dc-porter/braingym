## Summary of the design

Bloom is an adaptive learning app: plain files, no build step, runs in Safari on an iPhone. The world is a celestial fantasy city of floating islands, working name Aurelune, where light runs on clear language. The learner arrives through the castle's dim portal gate, meets the anchor character Queen Amaris and repairs the gate's inscription with real writing practice. Each place has one resident host, two or more activity types and things to grow with coins. Coins come only from finished learning tasks. Small expansions cost coins. Big portals open at learning milestones. Part of the world is generated by code so it never runs out.

Read 01-summary.md, then 02-challenges.md, then 09-plug-in-contract.md. The rest is reference.

## Rules that are not negotiable

1. **Lessons are never gated.** Nothing in the world may stand between the learner and a lesson.
2. **Coins come only from finished learning tasks.** The world can spend coins and can never add them.
3. **Never punish.** Nothing earned is taken away. Nothing decays, dims again, expires or dies. No streaks. No timers on answers. No guilt in any message.
4. **The placement test is pure measurement.** The world never serves a placement item, never shows feedback during the test and never lets a break game touch a placement score.
5. **Every activity names a sub-skill.** No skill, no activity.
6. **The world never reads or writes saved data directly,** never grades and never sets difficulty. It asks Bloom through the doorway.
7. **Do not change how the existing app behaves.** The world is added alongside.
8. **The Never build list in the Design Guide applies in full.**
9. **Every character, building, creature and name is original.** Nothing from any franchise. No character resembles a real person. Skin tones are color values only.
10. **Quantum ideas are visual only.** Nothing teaches physics.
11. **Do not fix anything from the audit unless a step below says so.**

**Rules of the look**

- Gold means earned. Dim means waiting, never broken.
- Tall slender shapes, crescents, rings, soft edges.
- Two lights, an edge glow on everything, no real shadows, glow faked with halos, distance haze.
- Motion is slow. Fast motion only for the one-second payoff.
- Characters: tall adult proportions, floor-length robes, gliding, drifting ribbon hair, simple calm faces.
- Text only on solid panels. Body text at least 17 px and following the iPhone's text size. Buttons at least 48 px, key ones 56 px, in the lower third.
- No red. Right is gold with a check. Not-yet is blue with an arrow. Never color alone.
- Speech: two short sentences at most, always skippable.
- Sound off by default. Reduce motion follows the iPhone and has its own switch.

## Performance rules

Full version: 10-technical-plan.md.

- 60 frames a second is the goal. Never below 30.
- Draw at no more than 2× the screen's point size.
- Under 80 objects drawn per frame, under 100,000 triangles, under 2,000 particles.
- Two lights. No real shadows. No full-screen effects by default.
- Stop drawing when a task card is open or the page is hidden.
- Repeated things are drawn as one batch.
- No image files unless there is no other way.
- Everything is built from recipes in code, so the scene can be rebuilt if iOS drops it.
- Task card, speech and buttons are normal page elements over the 3D picture.
- The 3D library version is pinned. Update it on purpose, in its own run.
- If the lowest quality step cannot hold 30 on an iPhone 14, stop and tell Dee. Do not keep polishing.
